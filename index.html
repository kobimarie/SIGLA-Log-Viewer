    Exercise guide

    Sample guide. Follow the exercises, number of repetitions and session length set by your physical therapist. Stop and ask your therapist if anything hurts.

    Before you start
    1. Sit in a chair with your forearm resting on a table, palm facing down.
    2. Clean the skin on the upper forearm, near the elbow, with an alcohol wipe and let it dry.
    3. Attach the electrodes where your therapist marked them: one sensor on top of the forearm (wrist extensor) and one on the palm side (wrist flexor).
    4. Strap the SIGLA unit near the wrist and keep the cables taped along the arm.
    5. Sit away from electric fans, chargers and other plugged-in appliances.
    6. Turn on SIGLA and wait for Signal OK. If a message appears, follow it, then try again.
    7. Follow the calibration steps on the screen: relax, then lift your hand back as hard as is comfortable.
    What the lights mean
    • Green: only the target muscle is working. This is the goal.
    • Yellow: co-contraction. Both muscles are working against each other.
    • Red: mostly the palm-side muscle is working.
    • Red blinking: a signal problem. Read the message on the screen.
    • A repetition counts only when you hold green for 2 seconds.
    Exercises
    1. Wrist lift and hold10 reps · 3 sets
    Lift your hand back slowly, as if waving "stop", and hold for 3 seconds while the light is green. Relax fully before the next repetition.
    2. Slow wrist lifts10 reps · 3 sets
    Lift the hand back slowly over 3 seconds, then lower it slowly over 3 seconds. Try to keep the light green on the way up.
    3. Contract, then relax10 reps · 2 sets
    Lift the hand back until the light turns green, then let the whole arm go loose until the device shows Rest. This trains relaxing as well as activating.
    4. Gentle effort practice10 reps · 2 sets
    Lift the hand back using only half your effort. Find the smallest effort that still turns the light green.

    If you cannot move the hand yet, still try each exercise. SIGLA measures the muscle signal even when the movement is very small.

    Sample session: 45 minutes of practice
    1. 5 minSet up and calibrate. Attach the sensors, wait for Signal OK, and follow the calibration steps.
    2. 12 minExercise 1: wrist lift and hold, 3 sets of 10.
    3. 2 minRest with the arm supported.
    4. 12 minExercise 2: slow wrist lifts, 3 sets of 10.
    5. 2 minRest.
    6. 11 minExercise 3: contract, then relax, 2 sets of 10.
    7. 10 minExercise 4: gentle effort practice, 2 sets of 10.
    8. 3 minFinish. Remove the electrodes, check the skin, and let SIGLA save the session.

    Active practice: 45 minutes (the four exercise blocks), the recommended daily minimum of 45 minutes of active practice, 5 days a week. With set-up, rests and finishing, the whole session takes about 57 minutes. Your therapist may change this plan.

    If the light turns yellow (co-contraction)
    1. Use less effort. Pushing too hard often makes both muscles tighten.
    2. Relax the palm side of your forearm and fingers while lifting the hand back.
    3. Slow down. A slower lift gives you time to notice and let go of the tight muscle.
    4. Reset. Let the arm go fully loose until the screen shows Rest, then try again.
    5. Check your position. Keep the forearm resting on the table and the shoulder relaxed.
    6. Take a break if it happens several times in a row. SIGLA will suggest a 30-second rest.

    If the light stays yellow for most of the session, or across several sessions, tell your therapist. They may change the exercise or check the sensor placement.

    Stop and ask your therapist if
    • You feel pain, numbness or tingling, or the arm feels unusually tired.
    • The skin under the electrodes becomes red, itchy or sore.
    • You feel dizzy, unwell or out of breath.

    SIGLA is a practice aid, not a medical device. It does not replace your therapist's advice.
